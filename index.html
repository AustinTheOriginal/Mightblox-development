📖 How to Play

Movement

  • W A S D — move
  • Space — jump
  • Shift — crouch & raise shield
  • Mouse — look (click to lock for free spinning)

Combat

  • Hold left click — attack / mine (bow: hold to charge, release to fire)
  • Hold right click — eat steak; place TNT/block
  • 1–5, scroll, or tap a slot — switch item
  • Touch: left joystick moves; right side: tap = attack/place TNT, hold = mine or eat steak, bow hold = charge & release to fire, drag = look

Health

  • 10 hearts (20 HP) + hunger bar
  • Eat steak to refill hunger; full hunger regens health

Kits

  • Fighter: sword 7 dmg, axe 9 dmg (disables shields 5s)
  • Demolitionist: flame bow + infinite TNT carts (same-level hits = most damage)

Totem of Undying

  • Held in offhand — saves you from death once with a sparkle + 3 golden hearts

World

  • Bedrock (unbreakable) → 7 stone → 2 dirt → grass
  • Spawn in the marked Safe Zone, fight in the open arena with hills
